Behind the build.

A plain-English note on what the product is, what the alpha can demonstrate, and what would need to happen before real daycares rely on it.

CURRENT ALPHA

What the demo shows

  • A weekly booking view with a 40-dog daily capacity.
  • Sample vaccination status: red for expired or missing records, amber for expiry within 14 days.
  • A Today view with one-tap activity notes.
  • A Pupdate preview filtered to an individual sample dog.
  • Browser-local persistence for demo changes.

What it doesn't do yet

  • It does not provide secure user accounts or server-enforced owner privacy.
  • It does not synchronise bookings between staff devices.
  • It does not receive or send WhatsApp, Instagram, SMS or email messages.
  • Its waiting-list form is illustrative only. It does not submit or retain personal information.
  • The proposed €19/month price is a concept, not a purchasable subscription.

Why focus on independent dog daycares?

The product is intentionally aimed at independent daycare operators who juggle bookings, vaccination checks and owner updates while caring for dogs. It is not presented as an all-purpose system for every pet business.

What needs to happen before launch?

Build and test authentication, server-side permissions, shared storage, backups, reliable vaccination records, operational recovery and a real waiting-list process. Test with independent daycare owners and revise the workflow from their feedback before making production claims.

Photography

The landing page uses a dog photograph served by Unsplash. Check the image and current licensing terms before commercial deployment. The photo is illustrative, not a picture of a customer or a claimed product user.
